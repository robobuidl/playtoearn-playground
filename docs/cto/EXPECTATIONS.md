## The Playground in one minute

- **Games:** 10 endless single-player games that get harder until you fail, one or two controls, no text needed, our own names, art and sound. Heroes are the PlayToEarn mascots Teddy, Bull and Dragonwhale, never with the logo or any text on their clothes.
- **Tries:** per game per UTC day, 3 free tries (9 with Plus). After that a try costs 10 points on the website, or in the app also one rewarded ad (AdMob, verified on the server). No ads on the website, no ads for Plus members, an ad never gives points. No extra daily caps. Free practice runs are welcome.
- **Weekly leaderboards:** per game, ISO week starting Monday 00:00 UTC, best verified score counts. The top 100 share 5,000 points per game per week on the P2E-100 curve.
- **All-Games Leaderboard:** Trophies = 101 minus the reward rank in each game (rank 1 = 100, rank 100 = 1, unranked = 0), summed over all games. The top 100 share a fixed 25,000 points per week plus the Community Bonus.
- **Community Bonus:** 50% of the points spent on points tries in the same week, counted live into the All-Games reward pool. Players only read that it is "based on the weekly participation activity of the community"; the formula never leaves the server.
- **Payouts:** after a review of top entries, automatically credited. A prize needs a verified identity (wallet signature and/or email, method chosen by the host). New accounts have a reward lock of at least 14 days. No region restrictions inside the game system; the host checks at withdrawal.
- **Anti-cheat:** every game is deterministic and records the player's inputs, so the server replays each run and only the server's score counts. Server-side layers (issued runs, timing checks, score ceilings, duplicate detection, rate limits, human review before payout, clawback) are part of the integration.

## Interpretations we applied

- **D33 (14-day reward lock):** read as "accounts younger than 14 days do not receive Playground rewards until they are 14 days old", which replaces the specs' 7-day hold (EXP-R-32, EXP-R-35, EXP-AC-19). If the host counts the 14 days differently (for example from crediting), the host rule wins.
- **D32 (verify to claim):** the principle stays (a prize needs a verified identity), but the method and timing are the host's choice. The specs' 30-day claim window is one possible implementation.
- **D28 (no extra caps):** with no daily run ceiling, the volume a single account can play is bounded by tries, points and the hourly rate limits in the anti-cheat part.
- **D31 (live bonus):** the specs recommended announcing the bonus from the previous week for legal comfort; the owner chose the live same-week pool. During the week players see a rounded estimate; the final amount is fixed at settlement.
- **Determinism in Firefox too (EXP-G):** the specs deferred Firefox to a later milestone; we require it now because players use it and replay verification depends on identical results in every browser.

## Open points for the owner

- **Adult confirmation:** the specs add a one-time "I am 18 or older and accept the rules" step before the first ranked run, because the host terms say point activities are intended for adults. No owner decision covers it yet: keep or drop.
- **D33 reading:** confirm the reading above.

## Rules and economy

This part sets out the product rules and points economy of the PlayToEarn Playground: free, points and ad tries per game per day, the run rules, weekly per-game leaderboards and their rewards, the All-Games Leaderboard with Trophies, the Community Bonus, eligibility and payouts, Plus, and how points move through the ledger. Under D27 the CTO builds this system in his own stack, so each row (EXP-R-01 to EXP-R-45) is one statement that can be checked against an implementation, new or existing, and marked met, partly met or not met in a gap report. MUST rows are required outcomes: owner decisions, or what a decided rule needs to work safely. SHOULD rows are the specs' recommended design; an equivalent with a stated reason is fine. Optional ideas sit in the last section. Sources: owner decisions in `docs/OWNER-DECISIONS.md` (D1 to D40, a newer decision wins), rulings and addenda in `docs/spec/00-design-rulings.md` (plain R, A, B and C IDs), and rules of the detailed specs prefixed with the spec number (for example "01 TRY-10" or "02 PAY-A14"). RV-01 to RV-31 are the golden vectors in `docs/spec/01-rules-and-economy-vectors.md`: they give exact inputs and outputs for testing, but predate D27 to D40 in the places listed under "Where the detailed specs are outdated". A value marked "spec default" is configuration; a value set by an owner decision is fixed. Amounts are whole P2E Points; days and weeks are UTC.

### Recommended, not required

Optional ideas from spec 01 and the archived plan that the owner did not ask for. None of them may override a decision above.

- **Player-set daily points limit** (01 PAY-5): a player may cap their own daily points spending on tries (spec options 0, 50, 100 or 200 points, or none; a decrease applies at once, an increase after 24 hours). It is the player's choice, not a system cap, so it fits D28.
- **System daily points-try cap kept as a switch that is off** (01 PAY-4, `pricing.maxPointTriesPerDay` = null per D28): if the owner ever wants it, it bounds losses on a hijacked account without a rebuild (spec 01 suggested 30 tries, 300 points a day).
- **Community Bonus launch floor** (`bonus.floor`; 01 open decision 1 suggested 10,000 a week for the first 8 weeks): shows a bonus from week 1; less needed under currentWeek.
- **Weekly emission cap for a growing catalog** (`rewards.maxWeeklyGameEmission`, 01 open decision 4): set it before passing 20 games, since every live game adds 5,000 points a week.
- **Two-person approval for large payout weeks** (`settlement.approvalAbovePoints`, 01 SET-4, open decision 6): off at launch; spec 01 suggests 150,000 once two finance staff exist.
- **Practice on the current ranked course** after the player's first ranked run of it (`practice.weeklyCourseAfterRankedRun`, 01 RUN-12, open decision 5): lets honest players rehearse what modified clients can rehearse offline.
- **Practice teaser for logged-out visitors** (`practice.guestEnabled`; the archived plan suggested 3 runs a day).
- **All-Games options, off by default** (R5.3; 01 OVR-6, OVR-7, OVR-9): count only the best N games (`overall.countBestN`), scale Trophies on small boards (`overall.smallBoardScaling`), or split prizes equally for exact ties (`overall.finalTieBreak` = `splitPrize`).
- **Switches for counsel or store answers without a rebuild** (R11.2): ad tries stop counting for prizes (`ads.adTriesPrizeEligible`), and per-platform app switches for points tries and prizes (`app.<platform>.pointTriesEnabled`, `.prizesEnabled`). These are platform switches, not region rules (D34).

## Games, game runtime, art and audio, anti-cheat

This part sets what PlayToEarn expects from the Playground's games, the runtime they run on, their art and audio, and the anti-cheat that protects prize play. It serves two uses: building the Playground from these documents, or checking an existing Playground against them so that an AI can write a gap report. Each requirement row has a stable ID (EXP-G-nn for games, runtime, art and audio; EXP-AC-nn for anti-cheat), one testable sentence, a level (MUST, SHOULD or MAY, as in RFC 2119), its sources and what a reviewer should look for; a gap report should answer every row with met, partly met, missing or not applicable, plus evidence. Under owner decision D27 the games, the runtime and the in-game anti-cheat ship in the games repository, while server-side anti-cheat is the CTO's integration step. Where a row names one of our formats or packages (P2RP v1, `<p2e-arcade>`, `@pg/*`, `p2e-verify`), an existing system meets it with an equivalent that passes the same check. Numbers are the specs' defaults: most are configuration values that may be tuned with data, but a gap report should list every difference.

#### Residual risk

A verified replay proves only that some input stream produces the score under our rules and seed, not that a person played it in real time (spec 03 §11). Valid-replay bots on real, aged accounts with a verified payout identity remain the main residual risk, whether they play live or feed inputs computed offline on the known weekly course at real-time pace. The layers above make such a bot pay a try per run, pass challenges, play in real time, stay under the score envelopes, avoid sharing inputs with other accounts and survive a human watching the replay of every top entry; a well-built bot with human-like timing noise at strong-human level can still win individual prizes. Prize curves, mandatory review and the 90-day clawback bound the loss (spec 03 §11.8). Further steps exist (`perRun` seeds, segmented seeds, canary entities), but none closes the gap completely.

### Where the detailed specs are outdated or heavier than needed

- **Server parts of spec 03 (sections 10.2 to 11).** Addendum D-2 keeps the verifier HTTP API with HMAC signing, the sim registry service, the worker pool, the flag pipeline and the review queue out of the game build. They describe one way to meet EXP-AC-09 to EXP-AC-25, not the only one: the CTO may call the verifier library or CLI from his own service, as long as it runs the same sim bundles in a JavaScript runtime such as Node (R9.6).
- **Course checks and seed screening.** D-2 lets `checkCourse` return `{ ok: true }` until course screening is needed, so the weekly seed screen (DET-17, DET-G18, `/v1/seeds/check`, `course-stats.json`) is optional at first and plain CSPRNG seeds are acceptable. Each game file's acceptance row and spec 04 T2 still ask for `checkCourse` on 10,000 seeds, which is stricter than D-2.
- **Partial-run preservation** (SDK-PRS: snapshots every 900 ticks, an IndexedDB submit queue, a keepalive submit on page hide) MAY be minimal (D-2); without it a closed tab simply abandons the run.
- **Commitments and telemetry** (RPL-03, RPL-09, SEC-AC-13 to SEC-AC-16) MAY stay empty with their slots reserved (D-2). Without them only the two clock gates of EXP-AC-13 work, so EXP-AC-07 and EXP-AC-14 are the first additions worth making.
- **(M2) items** in specs 03 to 05 (oracle bots and envelopes, near-duplicate similarity, oracle timing, risk score, random audit, bot-based course check, the color-vision tool) are not needed for a first version. Firefox is the exception: spec 03 (DET-34, SDK-TK-50) and spec 04 (T1) tag it M2, but PLAN.md (P1 gate) and AGENTS.md require it now, and EXP-G-28 follows them.
- **Tunable numbers.** Thresholds in spec 03 §15 (rate limits, timing factors, feature thresholds) are starting defaults to recalibrate from shadow-mode data; a different configured value with a stated reason is not a gap.
- **Superseded text still in the specs.** R10.1 "Electric Flat" (now Mascot Universe, B3). R8.5 [confirm] Teddy (settled by D24 and B2). R12 and CMP-G02 ask for a trademark knockout search (D36: no search, rename on conflict). R1.2's daily ceiling of 10 ranked runs per game is gone (D28), yet spec 03 §11.8 and SEC-AC-50 still count on it, so a single account's daily run volume is now bounded only by tries, points and the hourly limits of EXP-AC-18. ## Platform, integration, UX and host security

This part sets out what the PlayToEarn Playground needs around the games: where its data lives and how points move, weekly settlement and payouts, the API and its concurrency guarantees, the native app and AdMob path, the games origin, Community Bonus secrecy, analytics and staff tools (EXP-P), the screens and flows players see (EXP-U), and the fixes the current host needs (EXP-S). Every row is one checkable statement, so the tables work both as a build brief and as a checklist for comparing an existing Playground system: a gap report can mark each ID as met, partly met or not met, with evidence. MUST rows are required outcomes. SHOULD rows name our recommended design; architecture, hosting and topology are the CTO's decisions (D40), so an equivalent design that meets the same MUST rows is fine. MAY rows are optional. Sources cite owner decisions in `docs/OWNER-DECISIONS.md` (D1 to D40, newest wins), rulings and addenda in `docs/spec/00-design-rulings.md` (plain R, A, B and C IDs), and rules or sections of the detailed specs in `docs/spec/` (prefixed 01, 02, 06 or 07, for example "02 PAY-A15" or "06 S9"); "res 01" is `docs/research/01-platform-and-market.md`. The specs hold the full design, SQL and test cases; the last section lists where they are outdated.

### Where the detailed specs are outdated

Decisions D27 to D40 came after specs 02, 06 and 07 were written. Where they disagree, the decision wins:

- **Jurisdiction gating (D34).** Spec 07 section 2.1 (`decideJurisdiction`, the `jurisdiction.*` presets, CMP-110 to CMP-116, vectors JV-01 to JV-08), the region clauses of its Official Rules template (item 2 blocklists, `pointTryRegionsText`), counsel item C3 and risk RK-2 no longer apply, nor does the plumbing that serves them: region gating through spec 02 `GeoAdapter` and `UserContext.region`, the `jurisdiction` column on runs and problem `REGION_BLOCKED`; spec 06 `ViewerView.region`, TryGate row 3 (`regionUnavailable`), the `state.region.*` and `elig.region` texts and the REGION_MISMATCH hold. Only ad availability may still vary by region and fill (D23), so the "no ad available" state stays.
- **Daily ceilings (D28).** There is no per-game run ceiling and no daily points-try cap (`tries.maxRankedRunsPerGamePerDay` and `pricing.maxPointTriesPerDay` are off). This drops the ceiling and points-try-count guards in spec 02 6.1, the ad-grant ceiling check (AD-A10, step C5), codes `CEILING_REACHED` and `POINT_TRIES_DAILY_CAP` and concurrency test C2; spec 06 TryGate row 9 (`ceilingReached`), `card.ceiling`, `gate.ceiling`, `pay.dailyCap`, `tries.runsLeft`, the ceiling sentences in `how.3` and `faq.plus.a` and UX-AC-GATE-3; and spec 07 framing F1 ("one ceiling for everyone"), the limits in Rules item 4 and placeholder `maxPointsPerDay`. Ad caps are not affected.
- **Payout identity details (D32).** D26 stays the principle, but the host picks the verification method per reward at withdrawal. The credit-time gate with `awaiting_identity` lines, a 30-day claim window and lapse (spec 02 6.6, P29, P30, I3, E11; spec 06 5.6, `reveal.claimLapsed` and the 7-day and 1-day reminders; spec 07 `identityText` and OD6) is one possible implementation, not a requirement.
- **Community Bonus timing (D31).** The bonus counts live into the same week (`bonus.basis = currentWeek`). The previous-week defaults in spec 02 (8.1 `bonus` phase), spec 06 (UX-BON-2 `announced`, "exact all week", the previous-week case of UX-AC-BON-1) and spec 07 (the config default, Rules bonus text A, counsel item C4 and risk RK-3) give way to the current-week path: a rounded hourly estimate during the week, the final amount at finalization (spec 01 BON-10) and Rules bonus text B.
- **New-account reward lock (D33).** At least 14 days, set by the host. Spec 07 Rules item 2 and spec 06 `elig.accountTooNew` assume a 7-day account-age hold (`eligibility.minAccountAgeDays`), and spec 02 PAY-A14 applies 14-day maturity only to accounts younger than 90 days or without a completed redemption.
- **Plus multiplier (D35).** Settled as off, so spec 07's "on" branch (`plusMultiplierText`) and the multiplier factor in the spec 02 PAY-A13 caps are not needed.
- **Scope and roles (D27, D40).** Spec 02 sections 1, 11, 14 and 15 (repository layout, demo app, test endpoints T1 to T10, conformance suites, fake host, milestones M1 and M2, porting kit) and spec 06 S20 (dev panel) describe a platform build we no longer deliver, so their absence from the CTO's system is not a gap. Spec 02 open decisions 1 to 3 and 7, R9.6 and spec 06 UX-ADM-0 (games origin, topology, admin origin, two-person staffing, first app with the bridge) are recommendations for the CTO to decide.
- **Ranked course policy (D29).** Weekly, daily or per-run courses and practice on the ranked course (spec 02 open decision 5, the spec 06 open decision on the ranked course, spec 07 OD5, Addendum C4) are the CTO's integration choice, not open owner questions.
- **Game names (D36).** No trademark knockout search: that step drops out of spec 07 CMP-302 and counsel item C12; games are renamed only if a conflict appears.
- **To confirm with the owner.** The one-time adult declaration and Rules acceptance before the first ranked run (A7; spec 06 UX-INFO-1; spec 07 CMP-006; problem `RULES_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED`) is a check inside the game system, which D19 and D34 avoid; keep it only if the owner or counsel asks for it.
